# Product: Seance
- Problem: beginners let AI agents write code they can't explain, and agents skip good practice.
- Users: beginner/junior devs, bootcamp students, fresh grads who code with AI.
- Product: CLI (`init`, `sync`, `quiz`, `review`, `mcp`) + web app with a small hosted API.
- Differentiator: quizzes grounded in a real AST structure map of what the AI just built, plus SM-2 review of missed concepts. Lead every demo with this.
- Hackathon: Kiroween 2026, Frankenstein category. Deadline Dec 4 2026, 2:00 PM PT.
- Out of scope: accounts/login, databases, non-JS/TS AST parsing, IDE extensions.
